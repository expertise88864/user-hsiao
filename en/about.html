About me

Dr. Min-Chien Hsiao

Min-Chien Hsiao, M.D. · Ophthalmology Resident

Ophthalmology lives at the millimeter scale. A 0.3 mm corneal epithelial defect can put someone out of work for three days; a retina can detach quietly without pain. This site distills the three most common clinic questions — red eyes, eye strain, blurred vision — into readable notes, so you can come to your next visit informed.
