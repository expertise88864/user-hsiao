Monitoring Childhood Myopia: SER or Axial Length? A 2026 Meta-Analysis Perspective

Published March 2026 in Am J Ophthalmol (Clark & Wong; 70 population-based studies): SER should be the primary monitoring metric for most children; AL should be reserved for the minority entering the long-eye tail.
