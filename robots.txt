# ========== AI policy (v35 — opened to AI crawlers) ==========
# Goal: maximize AI / answer-engine visibility and citation for a young site.
# ALL AI / LLM crawlers are now permitted. With no opt-out group of their own,
# they fall under the wildcard (*) policy at the top of this file, which lets
# them crawl public content while the private /admin, /api and /reset-sw paths
# stay out of crawl space. This covers both:
#   1. query-time citation agents (ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot,
#      Claude-User, ClaudeBot — explicit groups above), and
#   2. bulk training / aggregation crawlers (GPTBot, CCBot, anthropic-ai,
#      Google-Extended, cohere-ai, Amazonbot, FacebookBot, Bytespider,
#      ImagesiftBot, Diffbot) — previously opted out, now permitted.
# Trade-off accepted: training crawlers send no direct clicks, but broader
# corpus presence helps the site/brand become known to LLMs over time.
# (SEO backlink crawlers below are throttled, not blocked.)
# ============================================================
